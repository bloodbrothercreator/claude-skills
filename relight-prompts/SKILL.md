# Relight prompts

Nine tested prompts that change only the lighting of an image. Each one keeps the person, face, clothes, pose, framing and background, so use them in an image editor that edits an existing picture (Nano Banana 2, GPT Image 2 edit, Magnific, Adobe Firefly). Source: the "11 Relight Prompts" Google Doc (two of the eleven were repeats).

**How to use:** pick the mood, attach the image, paste the prompt. To adapt one, change the colours, the direction of the light or the strength, but always keep the "preserve…" sentence: that's what stops the editor redrawing the person. For thumbnails, pair with the `thumbnail-prompt` skill. For a new campaign look, start from `steal-like-an-artist`.

## 1. Lightning flash
Transform the lighting as if a distant lightning flash had just illuminated the scene. Preserve absolutely all original characteristics of the person and environment. Create a cold white-blue light coming from a high side direction, extremely defined and momentary, illuminating selected areas of the face and body while others remain in deep shadow. Add a subtle storm atmosphere through contrast and light temperature, without adding rain, visible lightning, or other elements. Cinematic, dramatic and realistic photography, strong shadows and highly directional lighting.

## 2. Blue moonlight, warm accent
Transform the lighting into a cinematic nighttime scene, completely preserving the person, face, physical features, clothing, pose, framing, and environment. Create a strong blue moonlight coming from the side, selectively illuminating the edges of the face and body while allowing the rest of the image to fall into deep shadows. Add only a subtle distant warm reflection on a few surfaces, creating contrast between cool blue and subtle amber. Dramatic, realistic, dimensional and sophisticated lighting, with clearly defined shadows and strong depth.

## 3. Cool cinematic with light beams
Relight the image with a cool cinematic base, maintaining a slightly cool tone as the dominant atmosphere. Allow the light to interact with the environment, illuminating the scene strongly and dramatically with intense highlights and deep shadows. Let the light spread across surfaces, creating realistic reflections on skin, clothing, and surroundings. Add subtle volumetric atmosphere with visible light beams to enhance depth and scale. Introduce controlled color tones like cool blue, purple, and slight warm accents, keeping a cinematic balance. Ensure the lighting feels physically integrated into the scene, not an overlay. Maintain a darker base for stronger contrast and visual impact. Keep color intensity balanced while allowing light sources to stand out. Preserve identity, pose, clothing, and composition accurately. Transform the lighting with dynamic light elements without redrawing the subject.

## 4. Bright daylight action
Relight the image with cool bright cinematic daylight lighting for an outdoor action scene. Natural ambient sky light combined with soft directional sunlight illuminating the face, arms, and front of the subject. Crisp highlights, balanced shadows, high contrast and clarity, making the subject pop against the background while preserving realistic outdoor textures, skin details, and urban environment atmosphere.

## 5. Cyberpunk neon
Transform the lighting into a vibrant cyberpunk night scene with glowing neon lights. Preserve all original characteristics of the person, face, and environment. Create a contrasting dual-color neon lighting setup with intense cyan from one side and hot magenta/pink from the other, casting colorful reflections on the skin, clothes, and surrounding surfaces. Add moody urban nighttime atmosphere, high contrast, sharp glows, and dramatic cinematic depth without altering the subject's pose or features.

## 6. Red alarm
Transform the lighting into a high-tension emergency atmosphere with intense red alarm lighting. Completely preserve the person, face, physical features, clothing, pose, framing, and original environment. Create a strong, saturated crimson red light coming from a high side angle, casting dramatic highlights across the face and body while plunging the opposite side into deep, pitch-black shadows. Add subtle red ambient bounce light on nearby surfaces without washing out skin texture. High contrast, suspenseful, realistic, and cinematic thriller lighting.

## 7. Green sci-fi glow
Transform the lighting into an eerie, sci-fi atmosphere illuminated by a mysterious green glow. Completely preserve the person, face, physical features, clothing, pose, framing, and environment. Create a vivid emerald-green light source coming from slightly below and in front of the subject, casting uncanny upward highlights on the face, hands, and chest while the back and upper areas fall into dark, cold shadows. Add subtle green subsurface scattering on the skin and realistic reflections on nearby textures. High contrast, dramatic, and realistic cinematic lighting.

## 8. Night camera flash
Transform the lighting as if the subject was just captured at night by a powerful, direct camera flash. Completely preserve the person, face, physical features, clothing, pose, framing, and environment. Create a stark, bright neutral-white light coming directly from the front camera angle, illuminating the subject with crisp, punchy highlights and casting a sharp, dark drop shadow directly behind them. Let the distant background fall off into deep nighttime darkness. Raw, high-contrast, hyper-realistic photography with intense clarity.

## 9. Violet fantasy glow
Transform the lighting into a dramatic fantasy atmosphere with a glowing violet and magenta light source. Completely preserve the person, face, physical features, clothing, pose, framing, and environment. Create a rich purple-blue base shadow across the scene, contrasted by an intense, glowing magenta-violet light coming from the side at chest level, illuminating the contours of the face, jawline, and arms. Add realistic light wrap and subtle glowing reflections in the eyes and on clothing textures without redrawing the subject. High contrast, dimensional, and cinematic.
